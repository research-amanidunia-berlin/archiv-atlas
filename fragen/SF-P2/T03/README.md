# SF-P2-T03

What access do the evaluators get: continuous, read-only access to training pipelines, not just to individual checkpoints, without creating new paths for data exfiltration? What minimum shared access standard will labs grant so that reports are comparable across the industry, who drafts it, and what would it take for one evaluator team to work at two competing labs at once? What controlled access to reasoning traces (a model's step-by-step reasoning) do they get, and what may they publish from them without enabling unauthorised distillation, that is, training another model on those traces?

Part of the collected question [SF-P2](../../../fragen/SF-P2/).
