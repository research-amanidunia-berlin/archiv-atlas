# SF-P2-T05

What must an evaluator publish when a lab changes its safety framework, evaluation tooling or disclosure rules so that earlier commitments become less enforceable, for example by narrowing what counts as a reportable incident, and is the evaluator then protected from dismissal? And when evaluators continuously inspect AI agents doing autonomous research on model architectures and algorithms, which enforceable legal rules and measures against inadvertent knowledge transfer (a “cognitive quarantine”) ensure, during later audits and across staff turnover, that they do not carry proprietary algorithmic breakthroughs to competitors?
