# SF-H4-T01

Who becomes accountable for model weights (the learned parameters), safety reports and the findings of independent evaluators in the lab when a lab is sold, restructured or dissolved or abandons a training run, and is that chain published beforehand? Which safety and evaluation duties transfer when someone acquires a lab's models, weights or training pipeline, or a non-AI company buys a lab, and must a government be notified before the transfer closes? Which commitments survive and against whom, and is there any mechanism that outlives the company?
