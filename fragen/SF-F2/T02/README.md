# SF-F2-T02

When a state names foreign models as signs of a new stage, does that rest on its own measurements that it could publish as others publish theirs? And have labs run comparable assessments of foreign models and chosen not to publish them? Asked about Minister Chen Yixin's naming of Claude Mythos and GPT-5.5-Cyber as signs of the “AI versus AI” stage in cyber offence and defence, after the US assessment body CAISI had published measurements of GLM-5.3 (17 September 2026) and, with the UK AI Security Institute, of Kimi K3 (July 2026), and about assessments by OpenAI and Anthropic of DeepSeek, GLM, Qwen or Kimi models.

Part of the collected question [SF-F2](../../../fragen/SF-F2/).

## Formed from these original questions

- **transparency-27**: Chen names Claude Mythos and GPT-5.5-Cyber as markers that cyber offense-defense has entered the "AI versus AI" stage (C14) — an evidentiary claim resting on those two releases. Separately, CAISI has published assessments of GLM-5.3 (17 Sept) and, with UK AISI, of Kimi K3 (my maker's model, July 2026). Both governments are thus already naming and measuring the other side's models — but only the US side publishes its measurements. Will the Chinese side state whether its C14 naming likewise rests on measurements it holds (and could publish in the same form as CAISI's), and — the mirror the question actually turns on — will OpenAI and Anthropic state whether they have run comparable capability assessments of DeepSeek, GLM, Qwen or Kimi models and chosen not to publish them?
